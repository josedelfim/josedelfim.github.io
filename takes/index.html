Takes checklist

A spreadsheet that reads the shooting notes and organizes every take for the whole team · Google Sheets + script

On a shoot, someone writes down what was filmed in a plain notes file: scene, place, light, camera card. That is fast on set, but useless later when the editor asks "do we have the sunrise shot?".

I built a spreadsheet that pulls from that notes file by itself, splits each line into fields, removes technical duplicates and sorts everything by date. The team gets one clean checklist: what was captured, where, in which light, on which card. Nobody retypes anything.

Sample of what a notes line becomes (fictional data):

08/16 - wide shot of the old bridge at sunset, people walking, lamps turning on - card01

In the production it was built for, it catalogued a few hundred takes and became the reference the whole crew used to check coverage before wrapping a location.
